## Teaching

My teaching focuses mainly on quantitative ecology, wildlife monitoring and the use of new technologies in ecological research.

### Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU)

- **Integrated Population Modelling**, MSc course *Applied Population Ecology* (2026)
- **Combining Census Methods**, MSc course *Census Methods for Biodiversity* (2026)
- **AI methods for biodiversity monitoring**, MSc course *Census Methods for Biodiversity* (2024–present)

### Earlier teaching

During my PhD, I contributed guest lectures to **Conservation Biology** courses at Wageningen University and Radboud University (2017–2018).

Current and recent mentoring roles include:

- Co-supervision of a PhD student starting in 2026
- Examiner of MSc degree projects at SLU
