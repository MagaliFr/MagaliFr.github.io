## Current position

### 2025–present · Researcher
**Department of Wildlife, Fish and Environmental Studies, Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU), Sweden**

Research on wildlife monitoring and management using camera trapping, citizen science, computer vision/AI and quantitative ecology. My work also includes project development, student supervision and collaboration with wildlife-management stakeholders.

## Previous positions

### 2023–2025 · Postdoctoral researcher
**Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU), Sweden**

Research on camera trapping, AI and citizen science for wildlife monitoring and management.

### 2022–2023 · Ecological researcher
**Altenburg & Wymenga ecological research, The Netherlands**

Applied ecological research, project work, fieldwork, data analysis, student supervision and reporting on topics including meadow birds, shorebirds, small mustelids and geese.

### 2016–2022 · PhD researcher
**Netherlands Institute of Ecology (NIOO-KNAW) / Radboud University, The Netherlands**

My PhD investigated the causes of spatiotemporal variation in reproductive performance of Eurasian oystercatchers in a human-dominated landscape. The work combined long-term monitoring, citizen science and quantitative modelling.

Earlier research experience included work on wild boar, red deer, lynx, geese, bats and other bird species using a wide range of monitoring and field techniques.
