## Research and professional experience

### 2025–present · Researcher
**Department of Wildlife, Fish and Environmental Studies, Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU), Sweden**

Research on wildlife monitoring and management using camera trapping, citizen science, computer vision/AI and quantitative ecology. My work includes developing and coordinating research projects, analysing ecological and image-based monitoring data, supervising students and collaborating with researchers and wildlife-management stakeholders.

### 2023–2025 · Postdoctoral researcher
**Department of Wildlife, Fish and Environmental Studies, Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU), Sweden**

Research on the development and application of camera trapping, artificial intelligence and citizen science for wildlife monitoring and management. The work combined ecological monitoring with computer-vision approaches for extracting information from large wildlife-image datasets.

### 2022–2023 · Ecological researcher
**Altenburg & Wymenga Ecological Research, The Netherlands**

Applied ecological research on meadow birds, shorebirds, small mustelids and geese. My work included project coordination, ecological fieldwork, data analysis, student supervision and preparation of technical reports for research and management projects.

### 2016–2022 · PhD researcher
**Netherlands Institute of Ecology (NIOO-KNAW) / Radboud University, The Netherlands**

Research on the causes of spatiotemporal variation in reproductive performance of Eurasian oystercatchers in human-dominated landscapes.

The project combined monitoring of breeding success with catching, ringing and GPS tracking of oystercatchers in the Dutch Wadden Sea and Delta regions. I collected physiological and morphological measurements, worked extensively with citizen-science data and analysed long-term ecological datasets using approaches including structural equation modelling and Bayesian statistics.

### 2016 · Research assistant
**Institute for Terrestrial and Aquatic Wildlife Research, University of Veterinary Medicine Hannover, Germany**

Worked on research into red deer space use in Lower Saxony and the effects of hunting disturbance on geese and agricultural crop damage. Tasks included developing a research proposal for external funding, spatial and statistical analyses in GIS and R, and fieldwork involving the capture and collaring of geese.

### 2015–2016 · MSc research intern
**Wageningen Environmental Research, The Netherlands**

Investigated the effectiveness of non-invasive monitoring methods for measuring the use of wildlife crossing structures by ungulates in Hoge Veluwe National Park. The study compared camera traps, video surveillance and track pads and involved field monitoring, statistical analysis and scientific reporting.

### 2015 · MSc thesis researcher
**Wageningen University, The Netherlands / Institute for Terrestrial and Aquatic Wildlife Research, Germany**

Investigated environmental and physiological drivers of wild boar reproduction and the potential influence of hunting. I analysed a long-term dataset on wild boar reproduction and developed the results into scientific manuscripts, including work that resulted in a peer-reviewed publication.

### 2013 · Research assistant
**Institute for Terrestrial and Aquatic Wildlife Research, Germany**

Worked on a wild boar management project in Lower Saxony. Field activities included capturing and tracking wild boar using telemetry and investigating habitat use.

### 2012 · Bachelor thesis researcher
**Norwegian Institute for Nature Research (NINA), Norway**

Studied microhabitat characteristics of Eurasian lynx kill and resting sites in northern Norway. The project involved GPS tracking of lynx, locating kill and rest sites in the field and collecting detailed measurements of local habitat characteristics.

### 2011–2012 · Bachelor research intern
**Harz National Park, Germany**

Conducted a pilot study of microhabitat selection by Eurasian lynx at hunting sites. Fieldwork included assisting with lynx capture, VHF and GPS telemetry, locating kill and rest sites, snow tracking, habitat measurements and subsequent statistical analysis and reporting.

### 2010 · Research intern
**La Tirimbina Research and Education Centre, Costa Rica**

Contributed to a project estimating densities of tropical forest bird species. Activities included bird monitoring, GIS analysis and fieldwork, as well as assisting with bat mist-netting.
