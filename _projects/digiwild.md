**2026–2028 · Swedish Environmental Protection Agency / Viltvårdsfonden**

DigiWild develops approaches that combine **citizen science, camera trapping, artificial intelligence and quantitative ecology** to improve monitoring and management of Swedish ungulates.

The project builds on large-scale wildlife observations and aims to extract information relevant for population monitoring from digital data, including camera-trap images.

**Role:** Main applicant and project lead.
