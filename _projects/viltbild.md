Viltbild is a collaboration between **SLU and the Swedish Association for Hunting and Wildlife Management (Svenska Jägareförbundet)** to develop a national platform for collecting and organising camera-trap images contributed by hunters and other participants across Sweden.

My work within Viltbild focuses on the use of **computer vision for wildlife monitoring**, including methods for extracting information on ungulate sex and age from camera-trap images.

The long-term aim is to develop camera trapping and citizen science into scalable tools for monitoring Swedish game populations.
