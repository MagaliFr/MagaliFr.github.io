**2026–2028 · Nordic Board for Wildlife Research**

This project develops a **Nordic collaborative network for AI-enabled wildlife research**, bringing together researchers working with wildlife monitoring, camera traps, computer vision and related methods.

The network is intended to support exchange of methods, data, expertise and research ideas across Nordic countries and to strengthen collaboration around AI applications in wildlife research and management.
